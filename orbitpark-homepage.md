## Section 8 — FAQ

### H2
```
Frequently Asked Questions About Airport Parking
```

**Supporting Text:**
Everything you need to know about booking airport parking with Orbitpark. Can't find your answer? Call our team on 0203 XXX XXXX.

---

**Q: How far in advance should I book airport parking?**

We recommend booking as early as possible — ideally 2 to 4 weeks before your departure date. Early booking not only guarantees your space but also secures the lowest available price. Prices increase significantly closer to your travel date, especially during peak school holidays and summer months. That said, we do accept last-minute bookings subject to availability.

---

**Q: Is it cheaper to pre-book airport parking online?**

Yes — significantly. Pre-booking your airport parking online with Orbitpark can save you up to 70% compared to turning up and paying the gate price on the day. Airport walk-up parking is among the most expensive in the UK. Always pre-book.

---

**Q: What is Meet & Greet airport parking?**

Meet & Greet parking is the most convenient parking service available at UK airports. A professional, uniformed driver meets you directly at the terminal departure entrance, takes your car keys, and drives your vehicle to a secure, monitored compound. When you return from your trip, your car is brought back to the arrivals terminal. No shuttle buses. No long walks. Just door-to-door convenience.

---

**Q: Are your car parks safe and secure?**

Absolutely. Every car park on the Orbitpark platform holds Park Mark® accreditation — the UK Police-backed national standard for safer parking. All our partner facilities feature 24/7 CCTV monitoring, perimeter security fencing, ANPR camera systems, on-site security personnel, and full insurance cover for all vehicles.

---

**Q: Can I cancel my booking if my plans change?**

Yes. Most parking products booked through Orbitpark include free cancellation up to 48 hours before your scheduled drop-off time. A full refund will be processed back to your original payment method. Cancellation terms vary by product — full details are shown at the time of booking.

---

**Q: What happens if my flight is delayed?**

Don't worry — your parking booking remains valid regardless of flight delays. Simply contact our team on 0203 XXX XXXX or via WhatsApp and we will update your return time at no extra charge. We monitor flight delays for Meet & Greet bookings automatically.

---

**Q: Do I need to print my confirmation?**

No. Your booking confirmation is sent by email and contains your unique booking reference. You can show this on your phone at the car park entrance. We also recommend saving the confirmation to your phone before travelling in case of poor signal.

---

**Q: Can I book for someone else?**

Yes. You can book airport parking as a gift or on behalf of another person. Simply enter the traveller's vehicle registration and contact details during booking. The confirmation will be sent to the email address provided at checkout.
